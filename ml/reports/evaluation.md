Generated by `python -m ml.evaluate` from synthetic data. A flagged day means 'worth investigating', not proof of misuse.

- Test: 702 user-days (70 anomalous, prevalence 10.0%; inflated for evaluation)
- Threshold: 95% quantile of each role's training scores
